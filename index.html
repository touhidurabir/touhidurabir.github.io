About

Most of my work is the part users never see: architecture, APIs, data, tests, and the production support that follows.

Full-stack software engineer based in Dhaka, with 15 years of professional experience building production web applications and leading engineering teams since 2017.

Since April 2022 I have been a core developer at the Public Knowledge Project at Simon Fraser University, working on Open Journal Systems. OJS runs 58,000+ journals across 156 countries, so changes land in front of a very large installed base. I am among the top 10 contributors to both the OJS codebase and pkp-lib.

Alongside that I lead the architecture of Dentall Manager, a multi-tenant SaaS ERP used by 50+ dental and medical clinics in the Netherlands, with a team of eight engineers.

I have worked with North American and European clients throughout my career, remotely, across wide time-zone gaps. I am open to remote roles anywhere and to relocating with visa sponsorship.

What I do

Four things I keep getting hired for.

  1. 01

    Backend and API architecture

    Laravel and PHP for the last decade, with Symfony, Node and Rails alongside it. REST API design, database schema, queues and background processing, caching, and the test suites that keep it honest. I usually own the full cycle, from first requirement through to production support.

  2. 02

    Multi-tenant SaaS

    I designed the tenancy model, RBAC and core domain modules for a clinic ERP now used by 50+ practices in the Netherlands. That means I have already met the parts that get hard later: data isolation, per-tenant migrations, billing, and the obligations that come with handling patient data under GDPR.

  3. 03

    Open source maintenance

    Ongoing work on the most widely used open-source journal publishing platform, plus 15 packages of my own. Core modernization, infrastructure updates, bug fixes, security patches, feature work, documentation, and answering issues from people I will never meet. Working in a fully distributed open-source team changes how you write code, and it made me better at it.

  4. 04

    Engineering leadership

    Team lead since 2017, most recently for eight engineers. Scoping, technical direction, code review standards, hiring and mentoring. I have been the main technical contact for clients through full delivery cycles, which means I can talk to the people paying for the work as well as the people doing it.
