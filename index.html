About Me

Full-stack software engineer based in Dhaka, with 15 years of professional experience.

Full-stack software engineer based in Dhaka, with 15 years of professional experience building production web applications and leading engineering teams since 2017. Most of my work is backend: architecture, APIs, database design, testing, deployment, and the production support that follows.

Since April 2022 I have been a core developer at the Public Knowledge Project at Simon Fraser University, working on Open Journal Systems. I have worked with North American and European clients throughout my career, remotely, across wide time-zone gaps. I am open to remote roles anywhere and to relocating with visa sponsorship.
